# Core Index

## Purpose

The core domain holds the ideas your whole system stands on: the principles, definitions and models that every other domain relies on. If a note answers "what do we mean by this?" or "what is always true here?", it probably belongs in core.

## What belongs here

- Definitions and glossaries that other notes link to.
- Principles and decision rules that apply across projects.
- Mental models and frameworks you reuse, such as a planning loop or a review checklist.

## What does not

- Rules about how agents operate the vault (those live in `00-system`).
- Project-specific notes (those live in a linked project vault or the matching domain).
- Raw research or drafts (keep them in the domain they serve until they settle).

## Naming

Name a core note after the concept itself, singular and plain: `Decision Rule.md`, `Glossary.md`, `Planning Loop.md`. A concept that grows large becomes a hub with child notes named `Planning Loop - Operations.md` and so on.

## Example

`10-core/Planning Loop.md`, type `contract`: the five steps every task goes through (intake, plan, act, check, report), what each step must produce, and when a task may skip one.
