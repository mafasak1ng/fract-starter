# Business Index

## Purpose

The business domain holds the work that earns or costs money: clients, offers, pricing, plans, processes and the decisions behind them. It is where a project becomes a commitment.

## What belongs here

- Offers, pricing and the reasoning behind them.
- Repeatable business processes, written as procedures an agent can follow.
- Decisions with their date, the options considered and the outcome.

## What does not

- Private financial records, contracts with personal data, or client secrets: keep those outside a vault you might publish.
- Large project work (give it its own linked project vault).
- Technical how-to notes (that is `40-technical`).

## Naming

Name notes by the business object and its role: `Offer - Setup Service.md`, `Process - Client Onboarding.md`, `Decision - Pricing 2026.md`.

## Example

`60-business/Process - Client Onboarding.md`, type `operations`: each step from first contact to first delivery, the owner of each step, and what an agent may draft but never send.
