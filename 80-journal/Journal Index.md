# Journal Index

## Purpose

The journal domain is the session record of the vault: one note per working session, written at its close, so that the next session (yours or an agent's) can pick up exactly where the last one stopped. It is evidence, not memory: lessons worth keeping are promoted by hand into `20-cognitive`.

## What belongs here

- Session records, one per session, built from [[00-system/templates/Session Record Template|Session Record Template]] and closed with a `summary5`.
- The guide to the five-part close: [[80-journal/Summary5 Guide|Summary5 Guide]].
- An illustration of a finished record: [[80-journal/Session Record - Example|Session Record - Example]].

## What does not

- Plans and decisions that should outlive the session: move them to the domain they serve and link them from the record.
- Raw chat transcripts, secrets, or personal data.
- Edits to an old record's facts. A record is append-only: correct it with a dated note at the end, never by rewriting what happened.

## Naming

Name each record `Session Record - YYYY-MM-DD - Topic.md`, for example `Session Record - 2026-11-21 - Validator Fixes.md`. Two sessions on one day get different topics.
