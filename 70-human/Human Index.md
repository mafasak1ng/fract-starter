# Human Index

## Purpose

The human domain holds the human side of the system: your goals, roles, routines, skills you are building, people you work with (described by role) and how you want agents to treat you.

## What belongs here

- Goals and the plan to reach each one.
- Routines and working agreements between you and your agents.
- A skills record: what you can do, the evidence, and what you are learning.

## What does not

- Health, family, or other sensitive personal data you would not want an agent to read or a public repository to hold.
- Contact details of real people.
- Agent operating rules (those live in `00-system`).

## Naming

Name notes by the subject: `Goals 2027.md`, `Skills Record.md`, `Working Agreement.md`. Describe other people by role, not by name.

## Example

`70-human/Skills Record.md`, type `record`: each skill with its level, the evidence that supports it and the date it was last confirmed.
