# Creative Index

## Purpose

The creative domain holds creative work and the systems behind it: writing, image and video generation workflows, style guides, character sheets, prompts that produced a result you want again, and design references.

## What belongs here

- Workflows for generating images, video or audio, with settings that worked.
- Style guides and reference sheets that keep output consistent.
- Drafts and outlines of creative pieces.

## What does not

- The media files themselves (those live in `90-assets` and are linked from here).
- Client deliverables and contracts (that is `60-business`).
- Prompt theory that is not tied to a creative output (that is `20-cognitive`).

## Naming

Name a workflow after its output: `Workflow - Product Photo Cleanup.md`. Name a reference after its subject: `Style Guide - Brand Colours.md`.

## Example

`50-creative/Workflow - Consistent Character.md`, type `operations`: the steps, settings and checks that keep one character recognisable across many generated images.
